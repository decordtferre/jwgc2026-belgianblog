Wave was back over the task area in Aalen again this morning, setting the tone for another demanding day. Today's task turned into far more of a blue affair than yesterday's, with just one productive patch of cumulus breaking up the grind. Here's how Michiel, Edwige, and Wannes each got on.

===

## [Michiel](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1194864)

There was lovely wave again before the start, and I managed to climb even higher in it than yesterday, though I think pretty much everyone did. My last PEV ran out and the gaggle didn’t start, so I ended up alone in the first leg. The Czech pilots in their ASW-20s caught up with me, but tucking in behind them under the cloud streets let us reel in a few of the pilots further ahead. The final 130 km were flown entirely in blue, and I could feel the gaggle behind me closing the gap - which, as it turned out, they were. Still a solid speed overall, though everyone here is just incredibly fast. It's a real privilege to be racing against pilots of this level.

## [Edwige](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1195240)

What a day! I started off on my own but was soon joined by the French team, and we flew the first half of the task together - merci les copains 🫶🏼. I probably launched a bit too light for how strong the conditions turned out to be, which made it hard to stay with the others, but the sky ended up being far prettier than expected. My fastest task ever flown, and so much fun again.

## [Wannes](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1194648)

There was wave again before the launch, a bit less usable than yesterday but still good fun. After that it was blue all the way to the first turnpoint, then a few cumulus clouds that worked really well. The last leg turned blue again, and yeah, I landed out. And then you watch everyone else fly right past you 😭. On the bright side, I made some new friends: the police, fire brigade, and ambulance all turned up about two hours after I landed, arriving just five minutes after my own crew got to the field!
