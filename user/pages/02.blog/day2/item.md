When we woke up, it looked like it might only be a decent day ahead, but it turned out a lot better. The first few hours were mostly blue skies with a base of only 800m AGL. During the launch, all pilots had to wrestle with these tricky early conditions. Michiel and Edwige managed to get off the grid early, while Virginie took a slightly later launch.

After a little while, the ceiling rose rapidly, eventually peaking at 3,000m MSL. Excellent cumulus streets lined the Swabian Alb for most of the day, though the surrounding skies stayed completely blue. Everyone decided to task towards Klippeneck, and the speeds were impressive - all three pilots passed the 100 km/h mark, with Michiel logging a solid 400 km today!

Unfortunately, Wannes came down sick and wasn't fit to fly.

The atmosphere in camp has been great all day, and it only got better in the evening, when we hung out with the British, Czech, and Dutch teams. We even ended up going for a late night swim with the Brits. Everyone is hyped and looking forward to the start of the competition! Below some pictures of throughout the day!
