Today brought a tricky blue day to the championship, with weak-to-okay thermals and low points scattered throughout the flights for almost everyone on task. With little to no cumulus to mark the lift, pilots were left to work the terrain and gaggle tactics to find their way around, making for a day of patience as much as speed.

===

## [Michiel](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1203089)

Michiel's start was pushed back from the original plan, as the first 150 kilometres looked distinctly blue and called for a gaggle to tackle them safely. The gaggle he joined, however, turned out to be far larger than needed, dragging down the pace of the opening leg and allowing pilots who had started later to close the gap, swelling the group even further. Around the midpoint, Michiel broke away, sticking with the blue a little longer to follow a promising forest line. Flying solo again felt good, even if it meant working down to a low save at one point, and the move paid off with a solid chunk of time gained. Once under the cumulus, progress picked up noticeably, though the thermals lacked the rocket-like strength of previous days. Michiel pushed to recover more speed on the final leg, and despite feeling frustrated in the moment, the flight turned out better than it felt at the time - good enough for 6th place on the day.

## [Edwige](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1203633)

It was a long day in the blue for Edwige, spending 200 kilometres chasing the gaggle from below before finally closing the gap. Just as things looked to be coming together, a climb was missed, leading to a more conservative flight and a hard fight to get home. On the bright side, it was good practice working weak thermals in the Discus. Edwige finished 31st on the day.

## [Wannes](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1203320)

Conditions at launch were decent, with reasonable climbs but no cumulus yet, so the start was delayed while waiting for the line to open. After starting, Wannes took a different route than Michiel and lost contact with him, instead joining another gaggle for the mostly blue opening section with only a scattering of cumulus, a stretch that lasted until passing the airfield again. It wasn't the easiest going, but manageable. Once the cumulus arrived, the lift turned strong and high, lining up into one long street that allowed for a fast run. The final glide was a touch weaker but still comfortable. Overall, a fun and solid flight, closing out in 29th place.
