After a long briefing, the first training tasks were set. 2h AAT in club class, 200km speed task in standard class. 

Wannes decided not to fly, but he’s starting to get better. He’ll be in full shape once the comp starts. Michiel and Virginie took off for the AAT. Conditions were expected to be blue at the start, a bit of Cu on the alp again, but there was a rain zone coming in in the evening. Michiel didn’t want to fly too much due to the heat. He started together with some pilot of the dutch juniors. They expected blue conditions, but once crossing the startline, the first whisps started to form. Virginie started a bit later. The thermals were even stronger than yesterday, but the energy lines a bit more difficult to find as the convergence was not marked with bombastic Cu’s as yesterday. Flying club class gliders above 100kph is not something you can do everyday. We flew extremely fast, but so did the competition… Interesting days ahead.

In standard class, Edwige wanted to explore more of the eastern region. The task setting was not optimal for the given conditions, so she opted for the recon instead of the task. She got to experience the available energy lines along the Nördlinger Ries - krater, formed by a meteor crater 15 million years ago, about 24km in diameter. A stunning piece of landscape. Also due to the heat, she opted for ice cream and a cooldown in the swimming pool.

Team Belgium ended this day in paradise by enjoying the traditional opening of a barrel of local beer (taste test completed off course). 

Tomorrow looks like a smaller window. Wannes, Michiel and Virginie have scruteneering planned for the day. Some smaller flights will probably be made afterwards.
