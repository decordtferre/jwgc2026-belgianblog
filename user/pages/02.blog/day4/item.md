With the official opening of the Junior World Gliding Championships in Aalen just around the corner, the last few days before competition kick-off were a mix of waiting, rigging and chilling.

===

### Friday, July 31
The pilots made the call: no flying today. That meant Michiel's LS4 was sitting idle - so his crew Carl grabbed the chance to jump in and fly it himself for the day. For the rest of the team, the day started early anyway: Wannes had scrutineering at 8am, so everyone was up in good time to get the glider rigged.

### Saturday, August 1
Another no-fly day, this time forced by the weather — thunderstorms were expected early in the afternoon, leaving only a two-hour window to fly at all. Not enough to make it worth it. Instead of sitting idle, Guillaume used the downtime to repair Edwige's water pump, one small but important job ticked off the list before the real competition begins.

### Sunday, August 2
Finally, the day everyone had been waiting for: the official opening ceremony. It turned into a full day out — starting with a train ride to Aalen to visit the monastery, followed by a bus into the town centre for the official opening of the competition. By 5pm, everyone was back on the bus to the airfield at Aalen Elchingen.

After three days of waiting, rigging, and ceremony, the team went to sleep early, because tomorrow it's finally time for the real thing: **Competition Day 1**.
