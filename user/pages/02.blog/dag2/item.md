**The silence over Aalen-Heidenheim was broken today by the sound of towplanes as the first unofficial training day got underway.**

===

After yesterday's arrival and rigging, today was all about getting airborne. The goal for the Belgian Junior Team was simple: get familiar with the local topography and the specific convergence lines that often form over the Swabian Alb. The weather played along, offering decent thermals and a clear view of the surrounding ridges.

Our pilots spent several hours in the air, testing the local airspaces and practicing their final glides back into Elchingen. It’s a technical area with some tricky wood sections, so every minute in the air now is an investment for the competition days ahead.

### Finding the Rhythm
Transitioning from the flatlands of Belgium to the ridges of Southern Germany requires a mental shift. The team spent the evening debriefing, comparing flight traces on SeeYou, and discussing the best tactical approaches for the local weather patterns.

### Ground Support
Behind the scenes, the crews have been working tirelessly to optimize the gliders. Water ballast systems were checked, batteries charged, and the "Belgian Village" at the camping site is now fully operational. The atmosphere is excellent—a mix of high focus and the typical Belgian camaraderie.

Tomorrow, the official registration continues, and we hope to squeeze in another long task if the clouds cooperate!
