Six flying days in, the team woke up to some welcome news this morning: a rest day was finally on the horizon. First, though, there was one more task to get through, and the forecast promised a mostly blue sky, with barely a cloud to mark where the thermals were.

===

## [Michiel](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1196926)

The plan was to start together as a gaggle, but after several other teams tried and abandoned false starts, Michiel and teammate Wannes ran out of PEVs and launched on their own, with Wouter Bakker of the Dutch team not far behind. Four small cumulus appeared near the end of the first leg - the only clouds of the day - before the sky turned fully blue again. Strong climbs and lift lines running along the forests kept the pace honest, but the final leg proved the toughest part of the flight: heavy sink dogged him nearly the whole way home, and once again the hardest thermal to find was the one needed to set up the final glide. Michiel made it round in the end - a solid way to close out six hard days of racing.

## [Edwige](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1199572)

Edwige had another good day in the air, up against a much bigger crowd at the start. The forecast had looked pessimistic that morning, but the day turned out far faster than expected: she completed the task at 111 km/h in the blue, a speed she never thought she'd manage under those conditions. A few cumulus popped up briefly over Aalen during the tow, but the sky went blue again soon after release, and the first climb took some searching to find. With the entire Standard Class packed into the same pre-start gaggle, she ended up leaving on her very last PEV, heading out alongside the German and Czech pilots. She was a touch too light to keep pace with them in the transitions, a challenge that's cropped up more than once this week, but made up the difference every time she found a thermal. A low moment over Klippeneck sent a ripple of concern through the Belgian ground crew, before a strong climb pulled her back up to the top and firmly back into the race. Every flight is teaching her a little more about her Discus, and the progress is starting to show - something she's determined to take as a win. The day wrapped up with a proper afternoon of air sports back at the airfield, a fitting way to celebrate before the well-earned rest day. Time to reset and recharge before the final push.

## [Wannes](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1197529)

Wannes had a really enjoyable day, with conditions holding almost entirely blue from start to finish. Teaming up with Michiel turned out to be the right call: the pair got away a touch earlier than most of the field and settled into a small group that kept a good pace throughout. Thermals were consistently strong too - round 3.5 to 4 m/s on every leg but the last, giving them plenty to work with. The final leg was the tough one: Wannes lost some power there, but still crossed the finish line just two minutes behind Michiel, a strong result given how much harder that last stretch turned out to be.
