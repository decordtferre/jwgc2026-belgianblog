Another day of racing in the books, and if there's one word that sums it up, it's rain. Showers moved across the course throughout the day, forcing our pilots into long glides between turnpoints and plenty of nervous altitude-watching. Here's how it went for each of them.

===

## [Michiel](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1191042)
Michiel had a day that basically told the same story three times over: long glides to almost every turnpoint as rain closed in, followed by anxious moments hoping he'd have enough height left to connect with a cumulus and climb back up. He wasn't the fastest around the course today, but as he put it, that hardly matters. Task complete, and already looking ahead to tonight's international evening.

## [Edwige](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1191168)
Edwige had her own adventure after landing out near Hermuthausen, where a group of kind strangers turned up with ice cream, a very welcome surprise after a long flight. Her only complaint: watching cumulus clouds start popping right over her glider just minutes after she'd already touched down. Classic gliding luck.

## [Wannes](https://www.weglide.org/flight/1191445)
Wannes had a tougher time of it. He waited too long at the second turnpoint for a rain shower to clear, and that hesitation cost him - he landed out just 400 metres short of final glide. Still, he covered a lot of ground on a difficult day. Looking back, he reckons he should have pushed straight through the rain toward the second turnpoint a bit sooner rather than waiting it out.

Once everyone was back on the ground, the day wrapped up with our international evening - fries, snacks, and beers, with a great atmosphere all around. Plenty of new friendships made with teams from other countries. A perfect way to close out a rainy, long-glide kind of day. On to the next one! 🇧🇪
