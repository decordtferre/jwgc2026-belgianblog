**The Belgian Junior Gliding Team has officially arrived at the airfield of Aalen-Heidenheim!**

===

After a long drive with trailers in tow, we’ve finally settled into the venue for this year's **Junior World Gliding Championships**. The airfield at Elchingen is buzzing with activity as teams from all over the world are unrigging their gliders and setting up camp.

The first impression of the German landscape is promising. With the Swabian Alb as our playground, we are looking forward to some challenging but rewarding thermal conditions. Today was all about "site seeing" from the ground: getting our accreditation sorted, finding the best spot for the trailers, and—most importantly: making new friends in the international gliding community!

### What’s next?
The gliders are being rigged as we speak. Tomorrow, we’ll take to the skies for our first unofficial practice flight to get a feel for the local topography and the airspace. The weather forecast looks decent, so we’re hoping to clock some hours before the official training starts on the 30th of July.

Stay tuned for daily updates, flight logs, and (hopefully) plenty of Belgian flags on the leaderboard!
